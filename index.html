About Akram Sabry / عن أكرم صبري

Akram Sabry is a Food Safety and Quality Management Specialist with over five years of experience in implementing, maintaining, and auditing Food Safety Management Systems within the dairy and chocolate manufacturing industries.

He is currently pursuing his Master's Degree in Quality Management at the Arab Academy for Science, Technology and Maritime Transport (AASTMT).

His professional expertise includes HACCP, ISO 22000, ISO 9001, GMP, GHP, BRCGS Food Safety, Rainforest Alliance Certification, internal audits, supplier evaluation, documentation control, risk assessment, CAPA, root cause analysis, and regulatory compliance with the Egyptian National Food Safety Authority (NFSA White List).

He is also a Registered and Approved Tutor by Highfield International, delivering professional food safety and quality management training programs and consulting services.
