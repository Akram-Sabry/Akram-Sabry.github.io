About Akram Sabry

Akram Sabry is a Food Safety and Quality Management Specialist with more than three years of experience in implementing, maintaining, and auditing Food Safety Management Systems within the dairy and chocolate manufacturing industries.

His professional expertise includes HACCP, ISO 22000, ISO 9001, GMP, GHP, BRCGS Food Safety, internal auditing, supplier evaluation, documentation control, risk assessment, CAPA, root cause analysis, and regulatory compliance with the Egyptian National Food Safety Authority (NFSA).

He is also a Registered and Approved Tutor by Highfield International, with experience in delivering professional food safety and quality training programs.
