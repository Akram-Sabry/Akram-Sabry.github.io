PRACTICAL LANGUAGE FOR THE TEAM

Current food safety terms

Short working definitions, linked to official source material at the bottom of the page.

FSC

Food Safety Culture

Everyday behaviours and practices supported by leadership and workforce commitment, awareness, clear communication, and the resources needed for an effective system. [Codex]

Allergen Cross-contact

Unintended allergen transfer

The unintentional incorporation of an allergenic food or ingredient into food not intended to contain it. Controls may include segregation, effective cleaning, and accurate label information. [Codex] [FDA]

EMP

Environmental Monitoring Program

A risk-based plan for sampling relevant processing environments and surfaces to monitor contamination indicators and hygiene controls. Interpret results and trends with follow-up actions; it does not replace process controls or required product testing. [FDA]

FD

Food Defense

Protecting food from intentional adulteration or tampering. The appropriate plan depends on the facility and the regulatory scope that applies. [FDA]

Traceability

Lot and product tracing

Records that connect a lot to relevant movement and information across supply-chain steps, helping identify affected product and support withdrawal or recall when needed. [Codex]

CTE · KDE · FTL

Tracking events, data and FDA list

Under the U.S. Food Traceability Rule, CTEs are specified events with associated KDEs. Additional requirements apply to covered foods and entities on the FDA Food Traceability List, not universally to all products or countries. [FDA]

Climate context

Climate change in organizational context

The 2024 amendment does not assume climate change is material to every facility. Organizations assess its relevance to their context and interested-party requirements against the full amendment text. [ISO/IAF]

Practical note: This page is for general education, not a contractual or legal interpretation of standards. Always check the official edition, local requirements, and guidance from the applicable certification body or customer.
