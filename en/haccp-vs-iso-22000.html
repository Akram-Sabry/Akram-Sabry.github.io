HACCP vs ISO 22000

HACCP is a method for identifying and controlling hazards. ISO 22000 is a broader food safety management system that places hazard analysis inside a framework for responsibility, planning and improvement.

Prepared by Akram Sabry · Last reviewed: 12 November 2026

What is HACCP?

HACCP focuses on hazards in process steps, controls, monitoring and verification. Its strength is connecting a hazard to a practical control at the right point in the process.

What does ISO 22000 add?

ISO 22000 provides a management framework for context, responsibilities, objectives, communication, documented information, review and improvement. Hazard analysis works alongside prerequisite programmes and management controls.

Is one a replacement for the other?

No. Having HACCP does not automatically mean that a business operates ISO 22000. An ISO 22000 system includes hazard analysis as part of the management system. The right scope depends on the business objective and applicable customer or scheme requirements.

Where should a business start?

  • Understand the product, process and real hazards.
  • Define hygiene, storage, maintenance and pest-control programmes.
  • Build a HACCP plan the team can operate and verify.
  • Add management, review and improvement controls when the scope requires ISO 22000.
Practical rule: Do not start with the certificate. Start with the hazard, process and people, then choose the level of system that serves the facility.
