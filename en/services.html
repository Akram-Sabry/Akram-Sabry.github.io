Food Safety & Quality Consulting

Practical support shaped around your facility and its needs

Work starts by understanding your operation and goal, then defining a suitable scope, reviewing gaps, and outlining practical implementation and follow-up steps.

The scope is agreed after understanding the operation. Consulting does not issue certification or guarantee an inspection or audit outcome; decisions remain with the competent authority or certification body.

HACCP and ISO systems

Support to understand suitable system requirements, review the current position, and plan practical procedures, documents and records for implementation and review.

  • Define system scope and facility objectives.
  • Assess gaps and prioritize actions.
  • Develop procedures, records and clear responsibilities.
  • Support implementation, review and improvement within the agreed scope.
