Program overview

Egyptian National Food Safety Authority (NFSA) Requirements

Understanding requirements and preparing for oversight and inspection

An introduction to official information sources and practical inspection-readiness for food businesses, tailored to the activity and current applicable requirements.

Ask about this program on WhatsApp

Examples and exercises can be adapted to the establishment, participant level and client needs. Contact us to confirm the final content, dates, fees and delivery format before registration.

Who is this program for?

  • Quality and food-safety managers and supervisors in food businesses.
  • Establishment managers and staff responsible for operations, hygiene or records.
  • Compliance and inspection-readiness teams across food manufacturing, handling and food service.

What the program covers

  • Recognize official information sources relevant to the activity.
  • Identify operational-readiness and documentation areas that can be reviewed.
  • Organize records and note issues needing action or clarification.
  • Plan initial follow-up steps and confirm applicable requirements with the Authority.

Key topics

  1. Official information sources and the NFSA unified registration portal.
  2. Identifying the establishment’s activity and the requirements that need to be checked.
  3. Reviewing hygiene and hazard-control practices as general readiness context.
  4. Organizing records and reviewing operational practices relevant to the activity.
  5. Inspection preparation, documenting observations and following corrective actions.

Common question

Is this a complete official list of NFSA requirements?

No. This is an introductory page and general training outline, not a comprehensive legal checklist or a substitute for consulting the Authority. Requirements vary by establishment, activity and product, and may change, so confirm them with official sources and competent authorities.
