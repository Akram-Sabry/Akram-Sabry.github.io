Program overview

Prerequisite Programmes (PRPs)

Good Manufacturing, Hygiene and Storage Practices (GMP, GHP and GSP)

Explore how prerequisite programmes establish suitable operating and hygiene conditions for food safety, and how manufacturing, hygiene and storage practices can be adapted to the site, product and hazards.

Examples and exercises can be adapted to the establishment, participant level and client needs. Contact us to confirm the final content, dates, fees and delivery format before registration.

Who is this program for?

  • Personnel and supervisors in food manufacturing, processing and packaging facilities.
  • Quality and food-safety staff, including HACCP team members.
  • Food warehouse, transport, distribution and catering personnel.

What the program covers

  • Understand the role of prerequisite programmes and their general relationship to HACCP.
  • Distinguish practical examples of good manufacturing, hygiene and storage practices.
  • Recognize site- and product-related factors that influence practice selection and monitoring.
  • Identify examples of records and observations that help monitor implementation.

Key topics

  1. PRP fundamentals and their relationship to good hygiene practices and HACCP.
  2. GMP fundamentals: facilities, equipment, workflow, maintenance and process controls.
  3. GHP fundamentals: personal hygiene, cleaning and disinfection, contamination prevention and pest control.
  4. GSP fundamentals: receipt, storage conditions, segregation and stock handling.
  5. Selecting and monitoring practices for the operation and its hazards, and recognizing when HACCP analysis or official requirements need to be consulted.

Common question

Are PRPs alone enough to ensure food safety?

Prerequisite programmes establish suitable operating and hygiene conditions, but do not by themselves replace hazard analysis or other controls needed for the product, process and applicable requirements.
